FAQ

Common questions.

What is IPGuard?+

IPGuard is a technology-solutions company organised by sector, based in Lleida (Spain) with nationwide coverage. One team that builds custom software, artificial intelligence, computer vision and sensors, cybersecurity, networks, digital marketing and ecommerce for businesses.

What does IPGuard specialise in?+

Smart solutions: computer vision and sensors by sector (hospitality, industry, retail, logistics…), plus custom software and AI applied to each company's real data.

Where does IPGuard operate?+

Based in Lleida (Catalonia) with coverage across Spain, and projects with international reach too.

Does IPGuard offer on-premise AI?+

Yes. We can deploy AI on-premise so your data never leaves your company, with GDPR and NIS2 compliance.

Which sectors does it cover?+

Hospitality and restaurants, retail, industry, logistics, law firms and advisories, health, hotels, real estate, education and ecommerce, among others.
