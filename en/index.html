Success stories

Real cases: the problem and how we solved it.

Hospitality

Less food in the bin, thanks to cameras

The challenge. A hotel group was throwing away a lot of buffet food with no control and no way to know how much or why. That's money lost every day.

The solution. We installed computer-vision cameras that measure what's eaten and what's wasted, and flag what to refill and when. The manager sees it all on a clear dashboard and decides with data.

Computer visionLess waste, more margin

Retail

The whole shop protected, with no IT staff

The challenge. They handled customer and sales data but had no IT person and no real protection: an attack or a data loss would have stopped the business.

The solution. We set up their full security inside the shop (firewall, filtering, VPN and encrypted backups). Their data never leaves and everything is monitored, with nothing for them to worry about.

Managed cybersecurityBusiness protected 24/7

Commerce

The whole business in a single system

The solution. We unified sales, operations and data into one platform with 50+ connected tools. Now everything lives in one place and updates itself.
