Your company runs on a broken tech foundation (and you may not know it)

A dozen tools that don't talk to each other isn't just annoying: it's a broken foundation that costs you time, money and security. Here's how it gets fixed.

By IPGuard · 18 September 2026 · 1 min read

Most companies don't have a tools problem: they have too many. A CRM here, spreadsheets there, a billing app, a booking app, WhatsApp on someone's phone… Each one works on its own and none of them talk to each other.

The symptom: everything works… halfway

Information gets duplicated and you never know which copy is right. Processes live in people's heads, so if someone is out, work stops. And security depends on whoever remembers to apply it. That's not friction: it's risk.

The real problem: it's not a tool, it's the foundation

Adding one more app on top doesn't fix it; it makes it worse. The problem is in the foundations: there's no single source of truth for customers, processes and data, and no security layer protecting everything equally.

The fix: engineering, not installation

We build one central foundation —a single source of truth—, protect it at network level (antivirus, our own VPN and ransomware-proof encrypted backups) and connect the operational layers on top: CRM, communications, POS and an AI optimized to get more right while spending less. Corporate-grade engineering, for businesses of any size.
