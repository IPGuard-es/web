FAQ about Regulatory compliance

Does NIS2 apply to me?+

It depends on your sector and size. We check with you and tell you what obligations you have.

Is a GDPR document enough?+

No. GDPR requires real measures; an unimplemented PDF won't protect you in an inspection.

What is the AI Act?+

The European regulation governing AI use. We help you use it compliantly.

Do you prepare ISO 27001 audits?+

Yes. We set up the management system and leave the evidence ready to certify.

Do you do the technical side too?+

Yes, and that's the difference: we implement what regulation requires, not just document it.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the scope and what you need. We analyse your case and prepare a tailored quote, clear and with no obligation.

How long does it take?+

It depends on the project. We usually start with a useful first phase and grow from there; we give realistic timelines up front.

Do you work with companies across Spain?+

Yes. We're in Lleida and work with companies across Spain, remotely and on-site when needed.

Do you offer maintenance and support?+

Yes. We don't disappear after delivery: we maintain, evolve and provide ongoing support.

Do I own the data and the code?+

Always. Your data and your code are yours. We work with confidentiality and can sign an NDA.

Can you work on-premise?+

Yes. Whenever possible we deploy on-premise so your data never leaves your company, complying with GDPR.

How do we start?+

Tell us your idea or challenge with no obligation, we analyse it and propose the best solution and a tailored quote.

Can I start small and scale up?+

Yes, that's the recommended path: we begin with the highest-return part and expand in phases.

Which sectors do you work with?+

Many: hospitality, retail, industry, health, logistics, firms, real estate, education and more. We adapt the solution to your sector.

Do you integrate with my current systems (ERP, CRM…)?+

Yes. We connect with your ERP, CRM, website, WhatsApp or other tools so everything works together.

Are you GDPR compliant?+

Yes. We work with security by default and GDPR compliance; for projects that require it, we document and audit.
