AI that solves real tasks, trained on your company's data.

AI only works when it's connected to your business.

Most AI projects end up as a nice demo that never touches the company's real data or processes. We work the other way round: we start from a specific task that costs you time or money, and build the AI on your own data —your ERP, your CRM, your documentation— so it solves it end to end.

The result is AI that answers customers, classifies documents, inspects images or forecasts demand using your company's information, embedded where you already work and with costs under control. And if privacy is critical, we deploy it on-premise: your data never leaves your office.

FAQ

FAQ about Applied artificial intelligence

Do I need a lot of data to start?+

No. We start with what you already have; many solutions (RAG, vision, OCR) work without training a model from scratch.

Does the AI keep my data?+

No. We can deploy it on-premise or in your own cloud, and your data is not used to train third-party models.

How long does a project take?+

A first pilot is usually a matter of weeks, not months. We start with a concrete, measurable case and grow from there.

Does it integrate with what I use?+

Yes. We connect it to your ERP, CRM, web or WhatsApp via API, without changing how you work.

What about AI running costs?+

We keep them under control: we pick the right model for each task and optimise usage so they don't blow up.

How much does it cost?+

It depends on the scope and what you need. We analyse your case and prepare a tailored quote, clear and with no obligation.

How long does it take?+

It depends on the project. We usually start with a useful first phase and grow from there; we give realistic timelines up front.

Do you work with companies across Spain?+

Yes. We're in Lleida and work with companies across Spain, remotely and on-site when needed.

Do you offer maintenance and support?+

Yes. We don't disappear after delivery: we maintain, evolve and provide ongoing support.

Do I own the data and the code?+

Always. Your data and your code are yours. We work with confidentiality and can sign an NDA.

Can you work on-premise?+

Yes. Whenever possible we deploy on-premise so your data never leaves your company, complying with GDPR.

How do we start?+

Tell us your idea or challenge with no obligation, we analyse it and propose the best solution and a tailored quote.

Can I start small and scale up?+

Yes, that's the recommended path: we begin with the highest-return part and expand in phases.

Which sectors do you work with?+

Many: hospitality, retail, industry, health, logistics, firms, real estate, education and more. We adapt the solution to your sector.

Do you integrate with my current systems (ERP, CRM…)?+

Yes. We connect with your ERP, CRM, website, WhatsApp or other tools so everything works together.

Are you GDPR compliant?+

Yes. We work with security by default and GDPR compliance; for projects that require it, we document and audit.
