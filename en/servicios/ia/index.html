AI that solves real tasks, trained on your company's data.

AI only works when it's connected to your business.

Most AI projects end up as a nice demo that never touches the company's real data or processes. We work the other way round: we start from a specific task that costs you time or money, and build the AI on your own data —your ERP, your CRM, your documentation— so it solves it end to end.

The result is AI that answers customers, classifies documents, inspects images or forecasts demand using your company's information, embedded where you already work and with costs under control. And if privacy is critical, we deploy it on-premise: your data never leaves your office.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a lot of data to start?

No. We start with what you already have; many solutions (RAG, vision, OCR) work without training a model from scratch.

Does the AI keep my data?

No. We can deploy it on-premise or in your own cloud, and your data is not used to train third-party models.

How long does a project take?

A first pilot is usually a matter of weeks, not months. We start with a concrete, measurable case and grow from there.

Does it integrate with what I use?

Yes. We connect it to your ERP, CRM, web or WhatsApp via API, without changing how you work.

What about AI running costs?

We keep them under control: we pick the right model for each task and optimise usage so they don't blow up.
