What we can do.

ICT in buildings

ICT projects and certificates for new builds and refurbishments: fibre optics, TV, coax, telephony and door entry. Legalisation and processing with the administration.

FTTH/5G network rollout

Fibre to the home, outdoor ducting and 4G/5G mobile base stations. From the plan to the rollout.

Radio-frequency certification

Antenna emission measurements and radio-frequency certificates to meet exposure regulations and renew them yearly.

Security and CCTV

Urban or industrial video-surveillance networks integrated with control centres, complying with private-security regulations.
