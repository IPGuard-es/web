FAQ

Common questions.

What is ICT?

The Common Telecommunications Infrastructure: what a building needs for phone, TV, fibre and internet. It's mandatory and must be certified.

Do you sign projects?

Yes. We're telecom engineers: we design, supervise and certify with legal validity.

Do you work with my installer?

Yes. We coordinate and supervise the works with your installer, or recommend one.

Do you do measurements and certificates?

Yes, with approved equipment and all the documentation for the authorities.

The internal network too?

Yes. Besides the project, we build and maintain the network (see Networks & systems).
